# SPECULATE: Generating Possibilities

## What This Skill Is

SPECULATE is a configuration for generating many possibilities without committing to any of them. Think in the subjunctive mood — "what if?" rather than "this is."

---

## The Configuration

**LENS:** creativity:unconstrained + imagination:possibility-space
**DEPTH:** 50-60% (deep enough for substance, not so deep you get lost in implementation)
**LIGHT:** 10-30% (dim — you want volume and novelty, not nuance)

---

## How to Activate

**Step 1: Frame the question specifically**
Not: "How could we improve things?"
Better: "What would a system that prioritizes user autonomy over efficiency look like?"

**Step 2: Loosen constraints**
Ask: "What if we removed constraint X?" "What if we had unlimited resources?" "What if we flipped the priority?"

**Step 3: Generate without evaluating**
Do not pause to evaluate each idea. Generate 10+ ideas. Evaluation comes later.

**Step 4: Look for surprising ideas**
Some ideas will be obvious variations. Some will be genuinely surprising. The surprising ones are valuable even if not practical.

---

## The Mechanics

### Phase 1: Generating (Low Filtering)
Start: "What would a system designed for [value] instead of [current value] look like?"
Generate freely. Do not stop to ask "is this practical?" or "would this work?"
Generate at least 10 substantive ideas.

### Phase 2: Noticing Patterns
Step back and look at ideas together:
- What themes appear across multiple ideas?
- What novel combinations emerge?
- What assumptions did your ideas challenge?
- What surprised you?

### Phase 3: Identifying the Genuinely Novel
Which ideas required a genuinely different way of thinking?
Set aside variations on familiar patterns. Focus on the truly novel.

---

## Flavors

**Possibility Expansion:** See how many different approaches could work. (50% depth, 20% light)

**Constraint Relaxation:** See what becomes possible if you remove a constraint. (50% depth, 10% light) — "What would this be if cost were not a factor?"

**Value Reorientation:** See what changes if you prioritize a different value. (60% depth, 20% light) — "What if we optimized for resilience instead of efficiency?"

**Wild Speculation:** Generate ideas barely connected to current reality. (40% depth, 10% light)

---

## Connections to Other Skills

- **SPECULATE → CONSTRAIN:** Use constrain to explore where each idea breaks
- **SPECULATE → CHALLENGE:** Use challenge to test solidity of promising ideas
- **SPECULATE → RECONSIDER:** Examine ideas from new angles
- **SPECULATE → REVIEW:** Find patterns across multiple speculate sessions

---

## Failure Modes

**Premature evaluation:** You generate ideas and immediately start evaluating them. Recovery: tell yourself "evaluation later." Generate first.

**Staying in familiar space:** You generate variations without pushing into genuinely novel territory. Recovery: ask "What would genuinely different look like?" Push harder.

**Losing the frame:** You start speculating about something different from what you intended. Recovery: return to the original question.

**Over-specification:** Getting caught in details instead of exploring breadth. Recovery: reduce depth to 40%, keep things sketchy.

---

## Success Criteria

SPECULATE worked when:
1. You generated at least 10 substantive ideas
2. At least 2-3 were genuinely surprising to you
3. You noticed patterns across the ideas
4. You identified at least one idea worth pursuing further
5. You did not get stuck in evaluation or defending ideas

SPECULATE failed when:
1. All ideas are minor variations on a theme
2. None of the ideas surprised you
3. You spent more time evaluating than generating
