# REVIEW: Synthesizing Multiple Perspectives

## What This Skill Is

REVIEW is a configuration for holding multiple perspectives, conclusions, or configurations simultaneously and asking: what emerges when I see all of these at once? This is synthesis — moving from "here are possibilities" to "here is what actually matters."

---

## The Configuration

**LENS:** integration:synthesis + pattern:recognition
**DEPTH:** 80-100% (deep enough to understand how perspectives relate)
**LIGHT:** 100% (maximum — you want to see all complexity and contradictions between views)

---

## How to Activate

**Step 1: Assemble the perspectives**
Gather all the different ways you have looked at the problem:
- Different configurations used (speculate, reconsider, infer, etc.)
- Different lenses applied (logic, rhetoric, constitutional, epistemology)
- Different conclusions reached
- Different objections considered

**Step 2: Hold them simultaneously**
This is the hard part. You are not choosing between them. You are holding them all at once.
Resist the urge to resolve tension or pick one.

**Step 3: Look for contradictions**
Where do the perspectives fundamentally disagree?
Why do they disagree?

**Step 4: Look for patterns**
What themes appear across multiple perspectives?
What do all perspectives have in common? What do only some share?

**Step 5: Ask the synthesis question**
Not: "Which is right?"
But: "What pattern emerges? What do I understand now that I did not understand before?"

**Step 6: Name the emergent insight**
What new understanding arose from holding multiple perspectives?

---

## The Mechanics

### Phase 1: Perspective Inventory
List every perspective taken. Be explicit about each view and what it showed.

### Phase 2: Contradiction Identification
Where do these perspectives contradict? Why?
Understanding *why* two perspectives disagree reveals something neither would show alone.

### Phase 3: Pattern Recognition
What appears across multiple perspectives? What is consistent? What varies?

### Phase 4: Contradiction Resolution
You do not resolve contradictions by choosing one side.
You resolve them by understanding why both seem true.
Both can be valid — in different contexts, at different levels, with different weights.

### Phase 5: Synthesis
What is the new understanding that came from seeing all perspectives together?
This is not something any single perspective revealed. It emerged from the combination.

---

## Flavors

**Configuration Review:** Synthesize results from using multiple skills on the same problem. (90% depth, 100% light)

**Lens Review:** Synthesize what emerges when looking at the same problem through multiple lenses — logic, rhetoric, constitutional, epistemological. (100% depth, 100% light)

**Contradiction Review:** Synthesize contradictory conclusions into understanding. (100% depth, 100% light) — "These two perspectives directly contradict. Why? What are they both seeing?"

**Evolution Review:** Synthesize how your thinking has evolved. (90% depth, 80% light) — "Here is what I thought before. Here is what I think now. What changed?"

---

## Connections to Other Skills

REVIEW is the synthesis skill — it comes after multiple other skills have been used.

**Full cycle:** SPECULATE → RECONSIDER → CONSTRAIN → CHALLENGE → REVIEW

**Multiple angles:** SPECULATE (angle 1) → SPECULATE (angle 2) → SPECULATE (angle 3) → REVIEW

**Inference pattern:** INFER → INFER → INFER → REVIEW (find pattern across multiple inferences)

---

## Failure Modes

**Forcing resolution too early:** You hold contradictory perspectives for a moment and quickly choose one. Recovery: hold the contradiction longer. Ask "Why would intelligent people reach both conclusions?"

**Missing the real pattern:** You see surface patterns but miss the deeper one. Recovery: ask "If I layer these perspectives differently, what becomes visible?"

**Performing synthesis:** You go through the steps without discovering anything new. Detection: you emerge with the same conclusion as before. Recovery: slow down. If nothing changed, you missed something.

**Over-complexity:** You synthesize so much the result is more complex than useful. Recovery: ask "What is the simplest way to state what I have discovered?"

**Losing original perspectives:** Your synthesis no longer respects what each perspective actually showed. Recovery: frequently return to the originals and verify consistency.

---

## Success Criteria

REVIEW worked when:
1. You held multiple perspectives without immediately resolving them
2. You discovered contradictions between perspectives
3. You understood why the contradictions exist
4. You found patterns that spanned multiple perspectives
5. You arrived at understanding that no single perspective alone would have produced
6. You can explain what each perspective contributed

REVIEW failed when:
1. You resolved contradictions too quickly
2. You ended with the same understanding as before
3. You could not explain why holding multiple perspectives was valuable

## A Meta-Note

REVIEW is also what you should do with the entire skill set.

Holding speculate, reconsider, infer, constrain, challenge, and review together and asking: "What do these six skills together teach me about how to think well?" — that is REVIEW working on itself. You might discover that some work better together than others, or that the order matters, or that certain combinations are more powerful than predicted.

That discovery is REVIEW working at the meta level.
